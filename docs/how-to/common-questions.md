# How to answer common questions

Short recipes for getting specific answers out of the dashboard. Each recipe assumes you have the dashboard open. If not, start with the [tutorial](../tutorials/getting-started.md).

## How do I find which author uses a term most?

1. Select the term in the **Term** picker.
2. Open the **Author** filter and clear any existing selection.
3. Scroll to the **Author summary** table — it lists authors ranked by how often the term appears in their articles, normalised per 1,000 words.

The top of the list is rarely the most prolific author overall; it is the author with the highest *concentration* of that term.

## How do I see how coverage shifted after a specific date?

1. Pick the term.
2. On the timeline, click and drag to zoom into the months before the date you care about.
3. Read the average line for that window, then repeat for the months after.

Periods of war, elections, and major court rulings are visible as sharp inflections — they make useful natural reference points.

## How do I compare the two outlets directly?

The timeline already plots one line per outlet, but the **Results table** below it can be sorted by outlet too. To compare totals rather than rates, look at the small metrics box above the chart: it shows hits and rate side-by-side for each outlet under the current filter.

## How do I find articles co-authored across categories?

1. Clear all author filters.
2. Sort the **Results table** by the author column.
3. Look for rows where two or more author names appear. Hit counts are split fractionally across co-authors, so co-written articles will surface in multiple status categories.

## How do I export data for further analysis?

Two download buttons appear under the results table:

- **Public export** — metadata and term counts only. Safe to share.
- **Full export** — includes article text. Local use only; do not redistribute.

Use the public export for charts, write-ups, or sharing with collaborators. Use the full export only if you need the raw article text for qualitative reading.

## How do I check whether the Hebrew side missed an author I expected?

The two outlets use different scripts for author names, and not every Hebrew-named writer has been matched to an English equivalent yet. To see the current state of name matching, switch to the **Author Matching** page in the sidebar. It shows accepted aliases and candidates awaiting review.

A missing author is almost always a missing name-link, not a missing article.
